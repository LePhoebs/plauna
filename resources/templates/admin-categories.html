Delete Category: {{category.name}}

Are you sure you want to delete this category?

Set a destination folder to move emails of a category to a custom IMAP folder (e.g. Archive/Newsletters) instead of the default Categories/<Name>. Use your mail server's folder hierarchy separator. Leave blank to keep the default. If the destination is the same folder the mail is already in (e.g. INBOX), the mail is left in place instead of being moved. Existing emails are not moved.

Flag: Plauna marks every e-mail it files in the category with an Apple Mail flag of this color (other mail programs show a plain flag). To keep e-mails in the inbox and only flag them, use INBOX as the destination folder. When an e-mail is recategorized, its flag follows the new category; deleting the category or removing its flag color removes the flags. A new flag color only applies to e-mails filed from then on. "Flag existing e-mails" flags every e-mail of the category in the category's folder - including ones you unflagged to keep them.

Days until trash: Plauna moves the e-mails of the category to the trash folder of their account this many days after their date. Only e-mails in the category's own folder (its destination, or INBOX) are affected, never an archive or the sent folder. It checks every six hours. An e-mail is kept if you moved it to another folder or, when the category has a flag, if it has no flag - remove the flag to keep one. Plauna keeps the e-mail in its own database as a training example. Leave the field empty to keep the e-mails forever.
